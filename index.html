About Almas Aria

Experience built on reliability and service.

Established in early 2009, Almas Aria Ltd & Logistics Services is a registered Afghan business operating under Ministry of Commerce and Industry license No. 13314.

The company was founded to provide logistics services including vehicle leasing, transportation, fuel and lubricants, and trading services such as import and export of a wide range of products for the Afghan market.

Our operations are designed to respond to project requirements with professional personnel, careful management and scalable capacity. We maintain close relationships with clients to provide quality service and technical assurance.
